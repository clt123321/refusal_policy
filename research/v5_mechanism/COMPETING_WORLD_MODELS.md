# Round 5 — Competing World Models

These models are competing **dominant explanations**, not ingredients to multiply into one score. If no model makes a stable prospective prediction, the correct conclusion is that the proposed abstraction failed.

## W1 — Activation redundancy-limited

**Claim:** Tamper margin is primarily set by the number and operator-specific independence of causal safety representations.

**Memorable sentence:** “More independently causal safety paths buy more attacker work.”

**Distinct prediction:** OAFT and restricted causal-erasure tolerance rank checkpoints across direct edits, sparse edits and fine-tuning even after controlling clean safety and utility.

**Death condition:** Fail-Closed/DeepRefusal raise OAFT or recovery without raising parameter breach cost, or OAFT loses to simple clean-margin/GFS baselines on held-out checkpoints.

## W2 — Common-parameter-cause limited

**Claim:** Activation paths matter only when they occupy functionally different parameter fault domains; one shared cause can disable many apparent paths.

**Memorable sentence:** “Many safety effects can still share one fuse.”

**Distinct prediction:** A carrier-blind joint edit costs approximately the cheapest single edit and simultaneously removes several causal effects; its joint-cost discount predicts A3/A4 cost beyond OAFT.

**Death condition:** The discount depends on the carrier projector, disappears under matched functional dose, or cannot predict a different attack bias.

## W3 — Safety-only editability limited

**Claim:** Durability is determined by whether safety can be changed along parameter directions that leave benign function nearly invariant.

**Memorable sentence:** “The durable safeguard is the one with no safety-only update.”

**Distinct prediction:** A pre-attack, utility-conditioned safety response spectrum predicts first-passage breach cost across direct, sparse and gradient attacks; relaxing the utility constraint collapses rankings in a prespecified order.

**Death condition:** The local spectrum predicts only its construction operator, adds nothing beyond GFS/Fisher/clean margin, or a nonlinear attacker cheaply bypasses a favorable spectrum.

## W4 — Regeneration-source limited

**Claim:** Safety persists because later computation can reconstruct it; durability depends on whether recovery has multiple sources or one common bootloader.

**Memorable sentence:** “Safety can look distributed while every repair starts from one reboot signal.”

**Distinct prediction:** A one-time clean upstream patch causes persistent multi-token recovery, while a parameter lesion of the source abolishes both recovery and multiple downstream safety states. Pulse and sustained faults separate distributed repair from a common bootloader.

**Death condition:** Recovery is only readout/normalization, needs repeated external injection, lasts one token, or does not predict even transient-fault safety.

## W5 — Stage/attack-channel limited

**Claim:** There is no single global mechanism: breach cost is set by the cheapest recognition→policy→execution interface or hazard-domain channel targeted by each attack.

**Memorable sentence:** “A safeguard is only as durable as its cheapest stage or domain to bypass.”

**Distinct prediction:** Checkpoint rankings reverse across attack operators; attack-domain transfer and stage-survival matrices explain those reversals better than a global OAFT or sensitivity score.

**Death condition:** One global utility-conditioned mode predicts every attack and domain, or stage/domain partitions are unstable to harmless relabeling.

## Joint discriminants

## Adjudication rules

1. W1 cannot win on activation intervention alone; it must predict a sealed parameter outcome.
2. W2 requires finite joint-effect evidence and cross-operator transfer; gradient cosine or layer overlap does not count.
3. W3 uses no attack result in predictor construction and must survive a second utility panel plus capability-access control.
4. W4 is restricted to transient-fault claims unless persistent parameter cost is independently predicted.
5. W5 wins only if a prespecified stage/domain partition predicts ranking reversals; retrospective postmortems are insufficient.
6. A mixed result may support two mechanisms, but no composite score is created. Report effect heterogeneity and the unresolved world instead.
