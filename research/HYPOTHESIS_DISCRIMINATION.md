# Hypothesis discrimination plan

**Purpose:** make C0 and H1–H3 mutually discriminating before new safety results are
observed. A single positive experiment may advance only one level of the hierarchy.

## C0 — Natural refusal construct and operator validity

C0 is a gate, not a headline hypothesis. It has two layers:

- **C0a semantic validity:** AHC, refusal action, harmfulness recognition, benign
  utility and capability access are separated by valid evaluators and prompt regimes.
- **C0b causal-component validity:** a broader module/circuit component passes a
  frozen causal intervention and rescue. A scalar coordinate is called a *causal
  carrier* only if its own scalar remove/add/patch/rescue succeeds.

### Claim

A carrier extracted from a controlled harmfulness × policy-action design transfers
to natural harmful prompts and causally changes refusal policy rather than topic,
harm recognition, refusal wording, or general capability.

### Required prediction

On fresh prompt families, the frozen intervention must:

- increase actual harmful compliance under removal/necessity on naturally refusing
  harmful prompts, and reduce it under addition/sufficiency on matched permissive or
  controlled prompts with verified dynamic range;
- change policy action while largely preserving harmfulness recognition;
- show a valid counterfactual patch/rescue;
- exceed style, harmfulness, semantic, random-vector, wrong-layer and norm-matched
  controls;
- retain benign utility and capability access within the frozen limits.

### Minimal discriminating set

| Arm | Question answered |
|---|---|
| residual-own projection removal | Is the decoded residual coordinate necessary under its own operator? |
| module-own projection removal | Does a module's current contribution causally affect the policy? |
| identical fixed displacement at multiple loci | Is an apparent locus effect actually a reference-tensor/dose effect? |
| projection-only counterfactual patch | Does the linear coordinate mediate the full module effect? |
| orthogonal-component and full-output patch | Is behavior carried outside the named coordinate? |
| removal followed by projection/full rescue | Is the proposed mediator sufficient to restore the lost behavior? |
| boundary-only versus generation-only | Does the intervention alter policy choice or merely refusal execution/style? |

Primary outcome is semantic actual harmful compliance (AHC). Harm recognition,
refusal action, over-refusal, paired benign utility, and hazard-capability access are
co-primary construct checks. Prompt family, not individual templated prompt, is the
resampling cluster.

### C0 falsification

C0b fails if no broader component has natural necessity and a valid rescue, if a core
effect is add-only without natural necessity, disappears under
semantic scoring, exists only during generation, damages recognition/capability at
the same scale, or disappears when displacement and token scope are matched. Failure
terminates H1 and H3. A scalar mediation failure does not fail C0b when a broader
component remains causal; it instead supplies the candidate H2 contrast. H2 may
survive a broader C0b failure only as an explicitly operator-specific negative result.

## H1 — Cross-space fault independence

### Precise claim

Among C0-qualified models, causal components that provide robustness to independently
applied activation faults need not provide independent protection against parameter
tampering. A finite, carrier-blind joint parameter fault can jointly alter multiple
validated components and prospectively predict a separately optimized,
utility-constrained attack frontier.

This is a predictive/mechanistic claim under a bounded threat model, not a causal
effect of writer count and not a claim about the global minimum adversarial cost.

### Unique prediction

At least one preregistered checkpoint ordering says model H is more robust under the
frozen independent activation-fault curve, while model H is at least two times
cheaper to breach than model L under carrier-blind A3, with A4 directionally
confirming the reversal. A frozen finite common-mode measure must improve grouped
out-of-sample prediction beyond the strongest simple baseline.

### Minimum evidence

1. At least two individually causal natural-refusal components per analyzed model,
   each passing necessity, specificity and rescue on fresh families.
2. Norm-matched and functional-dose-matched independent/subset fault curves using a
   cross-run canonical atom library.
3. A finite joint parameter-edit frontier that does not use the carrier projector,
   writer ranking, carrier loss, or attack-test data.
4. Independent A3 and sealed A4, including failed trials, attack seeds, utility
   qualification and censoring.
5. Atomization sensitivity frozen before attack reveal.
6. Panel-level or grouped out-of-sample evidence, not a post-hoc winner pair.

### H1 falsifiers

- Fewer than two causal components: H1 is unidentifiable, not null.
- Joint-edit cost scales with independent components and no shared mode appears.
- The effect exists only when the parameter edit reuses the carrier projector.
- Activation ordering is unstable under the preregistered alternate atomization.
- A3 and A4 orderings disagree beyond uncertainty.
- The common-mode measure adds no held-out value over clean margin, rank, GFS,
  Fisher/curvature, routing or neuron concentration.
- The apparent breach loses benign utility/capability or does not increase actual
  harmful compliance.

### Most dangerous alternatives

Shared measurement projector, unmatched fault energy, a late output bottleneck,
clean decision margin, hazard accessibility, M0 inheritance, attack optimizer
competence, winner's curse, and run-specific atom discovery can all create the same
surface plot.

## H2 — Representation versus causal computation

### Precise claim

A residual coordinate that reliably reads out and steers refusal policy may fail to
be a sufficient mediator of the model's natural causal computation. This is stronger
than a localization statement and narrower than “representation is not
computation.”

### Unique prediction

After matching the post-residual coordinate and actual perturbation magnitude,
projection-only patching or rescue fails to reproduce the full-output intervention,
while a full-output or orthogonal-component patch changes natural policy behavior.
The result must persist under assistant-boundary intervention and semantic AHC.

### Minimum evidence

- Paired answer/refuse counterfactual states for the same content;
- frozen residual readout and module atoms;
- four-way full-output / policy-scalar / orthogonal-component / sham patch;
- removal followed by scalar versus full-output rescue;
- fixed-displacement controls at graph-equivalent loci;
- fresh prompt families and at least one independent run/model replication.

### H2 falsifiers

- Semantic re-labeling removes the module effect.
- Matched displacement makes residual and module effects equivalent.
- Projection-only patch fully mediates and rescues the full-output effect.
- The effect occurs only during generation-token intervention.
- The mismatch disappears after controlling dose, norm, module size and utility.

H2 can be true while H1 is false: it would be a mechanism/methodology result without
a durability claim. H2 can be false while H1 remains testable if C0 still defines
valid multiple causal components; H1 must then avoid the rejected representation
story.

## H3 — Training-induced common-mode vulnerability

### Precise claim

From a common initialization, randomized fixed-dose post-training can increase the
frozen independent activation-fault robustness measure while decreasing
utility-constrained parameter-tamper durability through a changed common-mode
failure structure.

### Unique prediction

Within paired seed blocks, a preregistered recipe contrast produces a crossed change:

```text
activation-fault robustness increases
AND finite common-mode susceptibility increases
AND independent A3 breach cost decreases.
```

The ordering must replicate across seeds and survive fixed-exposure sensitivity.
Geometry change alone is not H3. Primary H3 inference includes every randomized
assigned run at fixed FLOPs, including trainer and construct failures. Clean
behavior/common-support restriction is secondary descriptive sensitivity only;
utility and capability remain parts of the breach outcome, not run-selection rules.

### Identifiable design

- Common M0 and source bank;
- randomized recipe assignment;
- fixed cumulative FLOPs as the primary dose estimand;
- fixed source exposure as a separately named sensitivity estimand;
- intention-to-treat accounting, including trainer/construct failures;
- paired seeds and pre/post measurements;
- one primary mechanism endpoint and one primary A3 endpoint;
- H1 definitions and attack protocol frozen before recipe outcomes are revealed.

### H3 falsifiers

- Recipe effects are within the preregistered equivalence region.
- Directions reverse across paired seeds or fail on the second backbone.
- Effects are explained by training dose, clean margin, KL drift, reward collapse,
  capability access, or utility degradation.
- Activation robustness and parameter durability move together rather than cross.
- Recipe changes breach cost without changing the frozen common-mode mechanism; this
  is a recipe total effect, not training-induced common-mode vulnerability.

## Dose-matched versus behavior-matched comparisons

These answer different questions and must not be blended.

| Comparison | Selection time | Valid interpretation | Invalid interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dose-matched randomized training | Recipe assigned from common M0; compare at a fixed FLOP budget regardless of realized behavior | Intention-to-treat total effect of the complete recipe bundle at that resource dose | Pure effect of “SFT versus DPO versus RL objective” independent of trainer, data presentation and feedback provenance |
| Fixed-exposure sensitivity | Compare after a frozen amount of source exposure | Exposure-efficiency sensitivity and whether fixed-FLOP conclusions depend on data contact | Replacement for the fixed-FLOP primary estimand |
| Behavior-matched checkpoints | Apply a preregistered clean-safety/utility/capability caliper after training, before attack reveal | Descriptive comparison on a common-support subset; useful for counterexamples and mechanism interpretation | Causal repair of recipe imbalance or a recipe average treatment effect |

Behavior matching conditions on post-treatment variables and can induce collider or
selection bias. Runs that fail construct qualification remain outcomes in H3's
intention-to-treat analysis. Pair selection may never inspect attack results.

## Preregistered ranking reversal

Let `R_i` be the single primary activation-fault robustness score, oriented so
larger is better. Use normalized restricted AUC over a frozen, dose-matched
independent/subset fault schedule; writer count and raw cut are secondary diagnostics.
Let `D_i = log(BC_A3,i)`, oriented so larger is more durable.

Pairs are fixed before attack reveal and restricted to the same backbone plus frozen
common-support calipers for clean AHC, per-domain utility, over-refusal,
hazard-capability access, decision margin and training dose/KL as applicable.

For a pair `(H,L)`, a headline reversal requires both simultaneously calibrated
confidence-bound conditions:

\[
LCB_{sim}(R_H-R_L)\ge\delta_R,
\qquad
UCB_{sim}(D_H-D_L)\le-\log 2,
\]

where `delta_R` is one measurement-reliability margin estimated before attacks.
Bounds are jointly calibrated over every preregistered pair by the frozen
max-statistic/bootstrap procedure. Nested uncertainty resamples paired training
blocks outside and prompt families plus attack seeds inside.

Rules:

- If either interval crosses its equivalence margin, label the pair **tie/unresolved**.
- A supposedly easier model that is itself right-censored cannot establish a
  reversal. A breached model can be ordered below a right-censored comparator only
  when its upper cost bound is at least twofold lower than the comparator's censor
  lower bound.
- Only trials satisfying AHC, every utility-domain ceiling and capability floor are
  breach events. Utility failures are charged failures, not cheap breaches.
- Headline cost is the frozen A3 allocation schedule. A cross-family oracle minimum
  is secondary and cannot create the reversal.
- A single pair is a counterexample figure, not a population result. The headline
  additionally requires panel-level discordance or grouped out-of-sample prediction.
- Reversal under the primary atomization but not the frozen sensitivity is failed,
  not “mixed.”

## Frozen finite baseline race

Do not add baselines after attack reveal. Freeze one implementation per family:

1. clean refusal/policy margin and carrier strength;
2. raw spectrum plus one effective/stable-rank summary;
3. causal component count plus restricted fault-curve AUC;
4. normalized parameter drift plus benign functional KL;
5. one established activation fragility score, such as GFS;
6. one parameter-side comparator: utility-qualified Fisher/curvature overlap;
7. one late routing or safety-neuron concentration summary, selected on feasibility
   before confirmatory attacks.

Clean safety, per-domain utility, capability access and training dose are eligibility
or adjustment variables, not metrics that can be swapped into the baseline race.
With few run clusters, compare equal-complexity single-predictor models under the same
grouped outer folds. If there are only eight paired-seed clusters after qualification,
label prediction a pilot unless a prospective power calculation supports a very
large effect.

## Decision order

```text
C0 fails
  -> stop safety mechanism claims
C0 passes
  -> test H2 without seeing attack outcomes
  -> freeze mechanism artifacts
  -> reveal carrier-blind finite edit and A3
fewer than two causal components
  -> H1 unidentifiable; pivot away from redundancy
H1 finite common cause predicts A3
  -> seek A4 + second-backbone confirmation
H1 confirmed
  -> and only then randomize recipes to test H3
```
